# Save7 OS

Internal ops platform for Save7, a student-run organisation operating across university branches. This glossary defines the roles, org units, and navigation concepts that gate access throughout the app.

## Language

**Save7 OS**:
The product — this platform, live at `os.save7.org`, commissioned by Save7's Exco from the user and Gilbert Lieb jointly. **One system, one name**, before and after the restructuring currently being designed: there is no second product, and nothing in this glossary describes a rival to it. Where a sentence needs to separate the system as it stands from the model being designed for it, that is a difference of **version, not of system** — say "Save7 OS today" and "the target model" rather than reaching for a second name.
_Avoid_: Save7 Operations OS, Save7OS, the OS, Gilbert's system, Nerve Centre (which names the effort, not the product)

**Nerve Centre**:
The planning effort that designs Save7 OS's organisational structure, and the repo it lives in — its wayfinding map, the decision tickets under it, and the spec they become. Names **work, never software**: Nerve Centre holds no application code, and nothing a volunteer signs into is ever called this. So "the Nerve Centre role model" means the model this effort designs, while "the Save7 OS role model" means the one the product carries. Named after the retired Coda nerve-centre document that hub content is drawn from; spelled British, like the rest of this glossary.
_Avoid_: Nerve Center, the Nerve Centre app / platform / system, a second Save7 OS

**Branch**:
A Save7 chapter at one university (e.g. UCT, Stellenbosch, Wits, UP, UJ), and the **entry point** through which a volunteer joins the organisation. A volunteer recruited at a branch works under that branch's team, and every project they take on stays under that branch's Branch Management regardless of where the project itself runs. A branch is not compulsory — a volunteer recruited outside any university has no branch (see Volunteer). Open-ended table managed by National Exco alone — added, edited, or deleted outright; no admin status confers this. Carries a separate active/inactive status reflecting current activity/momentum, not whether it still exists — an inactive branch is still fully editable, just flagged as needing a push to get moving again.
_Avoid_: location, chapter, campus

**National Exco**:
Org-wide leadership seats — CEO, COO, CGO (Chief Growth Officer — field of responsibility still TBD), CFO. Full access to every hub across every branch and department. Provides overall oversight, alignment, and accountability across both the Branch and Department systems. Authors Department Head MOUs directly; reviews and signs off on Branch-Manager-authored Project Lead MOUs — routed to them by the General Liaison. Super Admins by default and irrevocably. Exco alone assigns every seat in the organisation — Exco seats, General Liaison, Department Heads and Branch Management — grants or revokes Super Admin status, adds branches and departments, and is the only seat that can delete a volunteer account outright. Exco also hold every branch-level power across every branch. No one outside Exco, whatever admin tier they hold, can create or alter an Exco seat or override an Exco decision. The four seats are **equal in permissions** — each sees and does everything Exco can. The CEO nonetheless carries the greatest authority within Exco, a matter of decision rights rather than a larger permission set, and does not leave by removal: a CEO steps down by Resolution, which may name their successor. In the OKRs Hub, Exco alone writes the Mission & strategy and the Pillars, and may author an Objective onto any unit — national, any branch, any Department. Their weekly meeting is the one meeting the platform records (see Meeting); Exco read every Meeting and the whole Action Point tracker, may log a meeting themselves as backstop to the General Liaison, and may tick any Action Point done.
_Avoid_: leadership, management, executives, exec team, CMO (no longer an Exco seat — see Department Head)

**Advisory board**:
Retained Exco capability, held by people who have served on National Exco. Not an honorary title and not a consulted body with rights of its own — an Advisory board member keeps working access to the organisation, in one of two states. **Advising**, the default, is full edit across every hub, every branch and every department — everything a sitting Exco member does day to day — minus the acts reserved to the sitting Exco: assigning seats, creating or altering an Exco seat, granting or revoking Super Admin, adding branches or departments, and deleting a volunteer account. Stepping down surrenders the power to decide who runs Save7 next; it surrenders nothing else. **Observing** is read everything, change nothing. Close to Super Admin in shape, but reached by seat rather than by admin status and so not identical to it — the Meetings section of the Compliance Hub is read by the advisory board and explicitly not by Super Admins. A member may step themselves down to Observing at any time; returning to Advising needs the CEO. Entry is never automatic — a Resolution places a retiring Exco member on the board, so an Exco member removed for non-performance does not reach it. Membership has no expiry but is not irrevocable: a Resolution removes it. Closed to anyone who has not served on National Exco. Members hold no standing place in Exco's meeting — the CEO invites them and the General Liaison routes the invitation — but they read its archive: the record, not the room. They do not read the Action Point tracker (see Meeting, Action Point). Underneath the seat they remain a Volunteer with their original branch, like everyone else.
_Avoid_: board, board of directors, advisory council, emeritus, former Exco (names the history, not the seat), advisory role

**General Liaison**:
Single org-wide seat, distinct from National Exco. The routing point between Exco and everyone reporting up to it: Branch-Manager-authored Project Lead MOUs pass through them on the way to Exco sign-off, and Department Heads' interval updates and any Exco-discussion requests (including meeting invites) go through them too. Full hub access across every branch and department, same as National Exco, but doesn't author or sign off MOUs. May author an Objective onto any unit — national, any branch, any Department — the only seat outside Exco with organisation-wide write, and a deliberate exception to the edit-own/read-others ceiling; this does not extend to the Mission & strategy or the Pillars, which are Exco's alone. Owns the lifecycle of volunteers who have no branch — acceptance, dormancy, deactivation, reactivation — since there is no Branch Management to hold it, with Exco as backstop, and helps a branchless volunteer with no Department author their Project Lead MOU. **Keeps the record of National Exco's meetings** — logs the Meeting, writes its Agenda Points, raises the Action Points off them, and works the organisation-wide Action Point tracker; Exco can do all of this too, as backstop.
_Avoid_: secretary general, secretary, exco liaison

**Admin Dashboard**:
Where organisation-wide administrative work is done — seat assignment, the Branch and Department lists, the master volunteer tracker — rather than a section of the hub nav, mirroring the way a Volunteer has their own dashboard instead of hub access. Reached by National Exco and Super Admins.
_Avoid_: admin hub, admin panel, control panel, back office

**Super Admin**:
The only admin status there is — a flag layered on a seat, not a seat of its own, and never held by a Volunteer. Confers **read** access to every hub across every branch and department — with **one carve-out: the Meetings section of the Compliance Hub**, which a Super Admin does not read. Maintaining the site is not a reason to sit in on Exco's deliberations, so that surface is reached by seat (National Exco, the General Liaison, the advisory board) and never by admin status. It confers no edit rights, which still come from the underlying seat. The "Super" is measured against National Exco rather than a lesser admin: a Super Admin sees everything and changes nothing structural. One who is not Exco cannot grant or revoke Super Admin, assign any seat, add a branch or department, create or alter an Exco seat, or override an Exco decision. Exco hold it by default and irrevocably, and the Tech Department Head holds it by seat, since maintaining the site requires seeing everything; otherwise granted at Exco's discretion, in practice to seats already carrying organisation-wide responsibility such as the General Liaison. Organisation-wide account upkeep that no Branch Management covers falls here.
_Avoid_: admin, superuser, root, owner

**Branch Manager**:
Runs one branch. Edit access to their own branch across hubs; read-only access to other branches. Authors MOUs (responsibilities/expectations/deliverables) for that branch's Project Leads — routed through the General Liaison for Exco review and sign-off. Appointed by Exco. With the Operations Manager and Finance Manager, accepts new volunteers onto the platform and grants Project Lead status — powers that come with the seat itself, not from any admin status. Branch Management own their branch's volunteers for the whole of that lifecycle: reviewing dormancy, deactivating, and reactivating. Deleting an account outright is Exco's alone. Any one of the three, acting alone, may author an Objective for their own branch — no quorum; other branches' Objectives are read-only to them, as elsewhere.

**Operations Manager**:
Runs one branch's operations team. Branch-scoped, same edit-own/read-others pattern as Branch Manager. Appointed by Exco; shares Branch Management's powers over their branch's volunteers — see Branch Manager. Oversees all volunteers in the branch, all Project Leads, and new-volunteer onboarding; along with the Branch Manager and COO, funnels volunteers with a specialised interest toward the matching Department.

**Finance Manager**:
Runs one branch's finance function. Branch-scoped, same edit-own/read-others pattern as Branch Manager and Operations Manager. Reports directly to the CFO — there is no national Finance Department standing between them. Appointed by Exco; shares Branch Management's powers over their branch's volunteers — see Branch Manager.
_Avoid_: branch function head, finance head, department head, branch lead

**Branch Management**:
The three branch seats **collectively** — Branch Manager, Operations Manager, Finance Manager — one set per branch. Not a seat of its own and not a fourth appointment: the name for the three acting as the branch's leadership, which is how the model refers to them whenever the point is the branch owning its people rather than one manager's particular remit. They accept volunteers, grant Project Lead status, and own their branch's volunteers through dormancy, deactivation and reactivation. Addressable as a unit — an Action Point may be assigned to a branch's Branch Management, which lands on all three.
_Avoid_: branch trio (the previous term, renamed), trio, branch leadership, branch exec

**Department**:
A national/organisation-level portfolio for specialised work: Media, Tech, Research, Education. Open-ended list managed by National Exco alone, extendable the same way Branch is. A **second supervisory axis**, not a label: joining a Department places a volunteer's work in that portfolio under its Department Head, while their branch's Branch Management continues to own them as a person. Distinct axis from the Branch system — operates directly under Exco, not under a Branch Manager. A volunteer may belong to several Departments at once, one interest not excluding another; each Head's authority reaches only their own portfolio's work, so two Heads never contend over the same task. Finance is not a Department — see Finance Manager.
_Avoid_: hub (a Department is an org unit; Hub is a nav section — a Department typically owns a Hub, but they aren't the same concept)

**Department Head**:
Owns, grows, and organises one Department at national scale. MOU authored directly by Exco (no Branch Manager in the loop). Gives Exco in-person or online updates at set intervals, and routes any Exco-discussion requests through the General Liaison. Becomes the national owner of the existing Hub matching their portfolio where one already exists (e.g. the Media Department Head owns the Media Hub — this absorbed the former CMO/Marketing remit, which is no longer a separate Exco seat or department). Holds real authority over their Department's work: sees what its members are doing, assigns it, and has a say in it; onboards them with the tools the portfolio needs, advises them, and mobilises them. That authority stops at the work — **the Department Head owns the work, Branch Management owns the person**, so acceptance onto the platform, dormancy review, deactivation and reactivation are never theirs. They may mark a member inactive *within the Department*, which is a per-Department status and touches nothing about the account. Authors their own Department's Objectives, as Branch Management authors their branch's; other Departments' are read-only to them, and the Pillars are not theirs to add or edit. One Head per Department and one Department per Head. Where a branchless volunteer in their Department seeks Project Lead status, the Head works the MOU up with them and passes it to the General Liaison. The Tech Department Head additionally carries Super Admin by seat.
_Avoid_: finance manager, branch lead

**Project Lead**:
Not a distinct account type — a status/upgrade on a Volunteer, granted via a Branch-Manager-authored MOU, scoped to a time-boxed initiative/project (vs. Finance Manager/Operations Manager, which are standing operational seats). Owns the resulting Project. Sees the volunteers working under them and their assigned tasks. Joining a Project Team confers nothing — only an accepted MOU makes a Project Lead.

**Project**:
A time-boxed initiative owned by a Project Lead, born when a volunteer's idea is written up as an MOU and signed off by Exco. Scoped either to a single branch, where only that branch's volunteers may join, or open beyond it, where the Project Lead controls which branches' volunteers — and whether branchless volunteers — may join.
_Avoid_: programme, campaign, initiative (use for the idea before it is signed off, not the Project)

**Project Team**:
The volunteers helping on a Project, plus the Project Lead who owns it. Team members remain ordinary volunteers under their own Branch Management; sitting on a team grants no status and no authority.
_Avoid_: project members, crew, squad

**MOU**:
An offline, signed agreement between a role and its supervising structure (Branch Manager ↔ Project Lead, Exco ↔ Department Head) setting responsibilities, expectations, deliverables, and KPIs/targets.

A Project Lead MOU is authored at the **lowest supervising level above the applicant** and routed upward through the General Liaison to Exco for sign-off; where the applicant already sits at or above a level, that level is skipped. So: a branch-onboarded volunteer's MOU is authored with their **home** Branch Manager whatever the project's reach, and Department membership does not divert it; a branchless volunteer in a Department works it up with that Department Head; a branchless volunteer in no Department works it up with the General Liaison; the General Liaison authors their own and goes straight to Exco; an Exco member authors their own and presents it to the rest of Exco. Not a UI workflow — referenced in the OKRs hub (KPI/target display only) and uploaded to the Compliance Hub for record-keeping.

**Resolution**:
A formal decision of National Exco, recorded in the Compliance Hub, carrying the CEO's approval. It is the instrument that moves the seats at the top of the organisation: removing an Exco member, placing a retiring one on the Advisory board or taking them off it, and a CEO's own stepping down, which may name a successor. Unlike an MOU, which is offline and describes a working relationship, a Resolution is held on the platform and changes who holds what.
_Avoid_: motion, vote, minutes, decision (too general — a Resolution is the recorded instrument, not any decision taken)

**Volunteer**:
The base layer every account holds. There is one door into Save7 OS (below), so everyone with an account is a Volunteer and every seat and status layers on top of it — Branch Management is a Volunteer holding that seat, and Project Lead and Super Admin layer the same way. Recruited at one branch, or at none. A volunteer normally enters through a branch and may transfer to another if they change university; a volunteer recruited outside any university has no branch at all — a small population in a student-driven organisation, owned by the General Liaison instead of Branch Management, and following the same paths into Departments and Projects as anyone else. Has a **Dashboard** — the same surface every member of the organisation has, not a consolation for holding no seat — plus the **OKRs Hub** — the one hub open to every member of the organisation, so that the ladder from day-to-day work up to the mission is visible to the people doing that work. No other hub appears in their nav. Can simultaneously belong to a branch, be a member of one or more Departments, sit on one or more Project Teams, and hold Project Lead status for one or more initiatives — these are not mutually exclusive. Accounts are not created on the platform: a prospective volunteer signs up and qualifies at learn.save7.org, receives a Save7 email address, and is then *accepted* onto the platform by their branch's Branch Management. Graduating does not end volunteering — accounts persist, and deletion is a genuine last resort rather than routine cleanup, reserved to Exco: an account costs a completed course and a Save7 email address to obtain, so destroying one is not a branch-level act.

**Hub**:
A top-nav section of the site (OKRs, Finance, Compliance, Media, Operations, Branch Managers, Departments — the last with a sub-hub per department). A hub is hidden entirely from a role's nav if that role can't access it, rather than shown disabled. The nav is **one level deep** everywhere except Departments: a hub's internal pages — the Meetings section of the Compliance Hub, for instance — are pages within it, not hubs and not sub-hubs. A Dashboard is not a hub either. **The OKRs Hub is the exception to that gating** — every member of the organisation can open it, Volunteers included, since a ladder nobody can see fails at its only job. What a member may *edit* there still comes from their seat.

**Season**:
A chapter of Save7's mission and strategy, declared by National Exco and written by them as free-form prose. Has **no fixed length** — not a calendar year, an academic year, or a cycle of any set duration; a new season begins when Exco decides there is a new one to write. Scopes the mission & strategy write-up **and nothing else**: Pillars, Objectives and Key Results carry no season and never roll over, so there is no archiving, carry-forward or closing-off anywhere beneath it. Seasons accumulate rather than overwrite and all stay visible, so that any member can see how the organisation's mission has changed over time; the latest is marked current.
_Avoid_: cycle, term, year, phase, quarter, sprint

**Pillar**:
An organisation-wide focus area defined by National Exco from the mission and strategy — standing examples: Public awareness, Help hospital systems, Help patients, Nimble operations. A **theme, not a commitment**: a Pillar has no owner, no Key Results and no progress of its own, and exists so that every Objective can be seen to serve something. Several stand at a time. Standing, not seasonal — revised in place rather than rewritten each Season. Retiring one **archives** it: removed from the list new Objectives may choose from, still displayed for Objectives already tagged to it.
_Avoid_: theme, focus area, goal, category, strategic priority

**Objective**:
A unit's commitment, standing on exactly one Pillar — the tag is mandatory, and an Objective that fits no Pillar means Exco needs a new Pillar, not that it should go untagged. Belongs to a **branch**, a **Department**, or the **nation**; the three are structurally identical and differ only in what they hang off. Belongs to a unit, never to a person, so a branchless volunteer needs no Objective of their own — they reach work through a Department or national Objective, or by being rostered onto any Objective. Carries an owner, and team tags where another part of the organisation's help is needed. **Bigger than any one champion** — written top-down by Branch Management, a Department Head, Exco or the General Liaison — which is what separates it from a Project, born bottom-up from one volunteer's MOU.
_Avoid_: goal, OKR, initiative, target, project

**Dashboard**:
The surface a person lands on when they sign in — **everyone has one**, whatever they hold, and it is the control centre for that account. Shows what is theirs: their open and overdue Action Points, the work assigned to them, who they are across the organisation (branch, Departments, Project Teams, Project Lead statuses), what they must act on by virtue of a seat, and announcements from Exco. **Per seat**, so what appears on it follows the person's duties — a Volunteer's and an Exco member's are the same surface carrying different things. Distinct from a Hub, which is organisational and shared; a Dashboard is personal. An Action Point is ticked done from here by its owner.
_Avoid_: home page, my work, personal hub, landing page (it is the landing page, but the term names the surface)

**Meeting**:
A recorded sitting of **National Exco**, the only meeting the platform keeps — weekly, though nothing depends on the cadence. Carries a title, a date and **attendance**, and holds Agenda Points. Attendance may include **guests** from elsewhere in the organisation, attached by Exco or the General Liaison; being named on attendance grants a guest no access to anything — not the minutes, not the agenda, not the record. Logged by the General Liaison, with Exco as backstop, and kept in the **Meetings section of the Compliance Hub**. Read by National Exco, the General Liaison and the advisory board; explicitly **not** by Super Admins. Branch, Department and Project meetings are not modelled.
_Avoid_: minutes (a Meeting holds them rather than being them), session, sitting

**Agenda Point**:
One item of business at a Meeting — a heading plus a free-text note of what was discussed or decided. Several per Meeting, each separate. Holds zero or more Action Points; an Agenda Point that produces none is ordinary, since plenty is settled in the room.
_Avoid_: agenda item, topic, minute

**Action Point**:
A piece of work somebody owes, with **owners**, a **due date**, and one **done** status. Owners are one person, several people, or a branch's **Branch Management**, which resolves to its three members at the moment of assignment. Shown **overdue**, in red, once the due date passes; nothing carries it forward and nothing copies it — it stays outstanding until it is done. Ticked done by an owner from their Dashboard, or by the General Liaison or any Exco member from the tracker, and the change reads the same everywhere at once. Reaches its owners on their Dashboard wherever they sit in the organisation, including people who may read nothing else about where it came from; whether an owner outside Exco sees the Agenda Point behind it is a per-Action-Point choice made by whoever raised it. **Has a source but does not require one** — a Meeting's Agenda Point is one source and a key result is expected to be another; an Action Point may stand alone.
_Avoid_: task (work assigned on a project board is a different thing), to-do, follow-up, item
